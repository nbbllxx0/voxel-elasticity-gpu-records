# T21: clamped beam with a fixed 8:1 interface, k = 8–64 (k = 128 extension)

Serves R1.3, R3.1 and R3.2 (Reviewer 3's point (1b), and (1a) for the corner and difference results).
The plan is `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` (frozen, unchanged). Departures are in `DEVIATIONS.md` (D1–D10; D8, D9 and D10 record
the corrections after the first, second and third checks, listed at the end of this report).
All tables below are generated by `t21_tables.py` (`out/report_tables.md`) from the result files:
`beam_interface.csv`, `uniform_controls.csv`, the two `*_sensitivity_D3.csv` files, `reference_checks.json`,
`verdicts.json` and `summary.json` (residuals of every kept vector: `terminal_residuals.csv`).

## Outcome in one paragraph

All prespecified solves ran: 8 confirmatory jobs and 14 extension parts, one GPU job at a time. Every job was
admitted with complete spill-watch coverage from the first watched sample and no spill (largest shared growth
110 MiB, limit 256 MiB). Two procedural problems decide how the outcome reads.

1. **(D3)** On most grids the frozen plan's coefficient-uncertainty rule cannot be met. GMRES-DC's first iterate
   below 5×10⁻¹⁰ already lies at the FP64 evaluation floor of the rounded operator, so the tightened iterate cannot
   halve the residual, and G-coef fails. This happened on 24 of the 28 coefficient solves at k = 8–64 whose reference
   was usable; the four solves of the k = 8 interface grid are the exception. This was seen on the development grid before any
   confirmatory solve. A sensitivity estimator was fixed then and is reported separately.
2. **(D7)** The authors' FP64 PCG diverges once it runs past its floor. Every tightened reference except one broke
   down; the exception is k = 8 interface, which converged in two iterations to a K0-ind residual of 9.97×10⁻¹². The
   k = 64 uniform and k = 128 uniform target references broke down too, so those grids have no reference.

Consequences:
* Under the frozen plan, the primary verdict (H1 + H2 at the interface) is **inconclusive** for both repaired forms,
  and so are H3 and H4. H5, the FP32 controls of the repaired forms at the interface, is **supported**: their
  compliance error is small and shows no sustained growth by H5's criterion.
* Under the D3 sensitivity, the primary verdict is **support** for both forms. The interface coefficient bias is flat
  over k = 8–64: 2.50–2.53×10⁻⁶ for the element-mean form and 3.86–3.88×10⁻⁷ for the difference form, resolved at
  every grid under D3's proportional-residual model. That model's uncertainty is not validated independently (see
  Limitations). H3 and H4 are supported on the interface layout and inconclusive overall, because the uniform k = 64
  grid has no reference.
* Every resolution at k = 8–64 also rests on the residual bound of the reference. That bound assumes the halved E29
  eigenvalue estimate is a lower bound on λmin, which the records do not establish (see Limitations).
* The k = 128 extension fails G-ref on both layouts. Its values are published with that label.

## What was run

* **Problem** (plan §2): Grid(8k, k, k), ν = 0.3, x = 0 clamp, consistent unit end traction in −y. Layouts
  `uniform` and `interface` (E = 1 | 1/8 by cell centre at x = 4), one FP32 moduli array per grid. The moduli are
  checked byte for byte after every part (all identical, `reference_checks.json → jobs[].moduli_checks`).
* **Operators**: K0 (node kernel, FP64) and K0-ind, a new independent element-by-element FP64 evaluation. K0-ind agrees
  with node_hoist on every grid to at most 3.9×10⁻¹⁶ of the largest entry, and with T20's CPU assembly on k = 4 to
  9.3×10⁻¹⁶ (`out/dev_check.json`). The four rounded-coefficient forms are T20's FreeOperators.
* **Development validation** (§10, k = 4, before any confirmatory job; `out/dev_check.json`, `out/launch_selftest.json`,
  `out/analysis_selftest.json`): every GMRES-DC, MINRES-DC, PCG-diagnostic and reference compliance agrees with SciPy
  direct solves of the CPU-assembled matrices within 1.2×10⁻¹¹ relative. The launcher self-test passes 11 cases. The
  analysis self-test passed 23 planted cases, including resolved growth, an unresolved value, a failed gate and a
  sign change. It now has 24: the added case checks that a failed coefficient solve reports its smallest-residual
  saved iterate (D8).
* **Confirmatory**: k = 8, 16, 32, 64, uniform then interface, one job each (`out/queue_confirm.log`).
* **Extension**: k = 128, uniform then interface, seven separately admitted parts each (`out/queue_extension.log`).
  GMRES(20) was admitted at k = 128 (predicted 16.7 GB against 22.6 GB free), so the fallback to GMRES(10) was not
  needed.
* **Pool cap and watch** per the authors' rule. Measured dedicated peaks were 0.46–2.31 GB for the confirmatory jobs
  and up to 14.9 GB for the k = 128 coefficient parts, all below their predictions, with one exception. The k = 128
  evaluation part measured 4.44 GB against a predicted 4.13 GB. The 1.5 GiB margin covered it and no memory was
  spilled. `guard.require_budget` agreed with every admission.

## Limitations

* The study covers one layout: a transverse 8:1 plane at x = 4 on voxel grids, plus the uniform companion. It says
  nothing about other contrasts, layouts or distorted elements. T33 covers the 10⁻⁹ voids and k = 128.
* The frozen plan's coefficient-uncertainty estimator does not work at the floor (D3). The resolved coefficient
  results above therefore rest on a sensitivity estimator. It was fixed before the confirmatory data, but it is not
  the plan's rule. It applies the plan's proportional-residual model across one outer step. In the 24 confirmatory
  cases where it replaces the plan's estimate, that step reduces the residual by a factor of 43 to 205. D3 gives a
  sensitivity analysis under that model; it does not validate the model's uncertainty. As a descriptive check, the
  observed change from the target to the later saved iterate exceeds the D3 compliance uncertainty in 2 of the 24
  cases and the D3 energy uncertainty in 1 (`summary.json → D3_model_check`, table M). The largest excess is the
  interface original stencil at k = 32: an observed compliance change of 2.21×10⁻¹³ against a D3 uncertainty of
  2.91×10⁻¹⁴, 7.6 times larger. Replacing each D3 uncertainty with the larger of the two keeps all 24 compliance and
  energy values resolved. This check is descriptive and changes no verdict.
* The reference uncertainty on every passing grid at k ≤ 64 uses the residual bound with λ_lb = 0.5 × E29's
  λmin estimate, divided by 8 at the interface. E29 estimated λmin by inverse iteration
  (`E29_review.py`, part R4). Its quotient wᵀw / wᵀK0⁻¹w is at least λmin, up to the inner-solve error. λ_lb is
  therefore a lower bound only if the true λmin is at least half the estimate, and the records do not establish this.
  Given a uniform lower bound, dividing by 8 is justified at the interface (K0(interface) ⪰ K0(uniform)/8). Every
  B_C and B_E, and every 10:1 resolution that rests on them, is therefore conditional on that assumption. This
  covers the compliance uncertainty of six of the seven passing confirmatory references and the energy uncertainty
  of all seven.
  The margin is given in `summary.json → lambda_sensitivity` (table L). The resolved compliance values stay resolved
  unless λ_lb is too large by a factor above 10⁸. For the resolved energy values the factor is at least 256.5 in the
  frozen pass. In the D3 pass it is at least 13.2, for the difference form's coefficient energy error at k = 64,
  interface (63 at k = 32). These values stay resolved as long as the true λmin is at least E29's estimate divided by
  26.4 and 127 respectively.
* No λ bound exists at k = 128. With the tightened solve diverging, no reference uncertainty could be established
  there.
* The uniform companion lacks k = 64 because its reference broke down. No fallback reference was computed (§13).
* The energy-norm error of the FP32 controls grows about 2× per halving. No test was prespecified for it (H2E covers
  the coefficient solutions only).

## Notes for T33 and the response

* T33's reference uses the same `pcg64`, so the same breakdown past the floor will occur. A reference that stops at
  stagnation, or restarts the search direction after replacement, needs to be in T33's plan before its data.
* A GMRES-DC tightening estimate needs a target iterate above the floor. Use the pre-target pair, or a target level
  clearly above the floor (e.g. 10⁻⁸), when planning T33.
* For the response to (1b), the honest statement: under the plan's frozen rule the coefficient results at the
  interface are not resolved beyond k = 8. Under the pre-data sensitivity rule, a proportional-residual model whose
  uncertainty is not independently validated, both repaired forms show a flat coefficient bias over k = 8–64,
  resolved by that rule: 2.5×10⁻⁶ for the mean form and 3.9×10⁻⁷ for the difference form. Every resolution is also
  conditional on the eigenvalue estimate behind the reference bound. The compliance error of the mean and difference
  FP32 controls stays below 3×10⁻⁶ with no sustained growth by H5's criterion (supported under both rules), although
  the medians rose by 1.44 and 1.61 from k = 32 to 64. The corner control's compliance error is not covered by that
  bound: it doubles per refinement to 3.19×10⁻⁴ at k = 64. The energy-norm error of the corner, mean and difference
  controls roughly doubles per refinement (observed orders 0.92–1.00 for mean and difference at the interface), which
  belongs in the text.
